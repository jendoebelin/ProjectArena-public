# Code of conduct

Project Arena is a game for everyone 13 and older, made by volunteers. The same rules apply on GitHub, on the
community servers and in voice chat.

**Do:** be friendly and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work (not the person), welcome newcomers,
and say so when you are joking in voice chat.

**Do not:** harass, threaten or insult anyone; use hate speech or slurs; make sexual comments or content; share
anyone's personal information; spam; cheat or break the game on purpose for others; pretend to be someone else.

**If something happens:** in the game use MENU > REPORT PLAYER (and MUTE); anywhere else email
vrsportsfan@gmail.com. Reports are read by the people who run the project and the official server and are kept
private.

**What we may do:** remind, remove comments or contributions, mute, or ban from the repository and the official
server, for as long as needed. Serious or repeated behaviour can lead to a permanent ban.
