Project Arena for PC VR (SteamVR, Meta Quest Link / Air Link, Windows Mixed Reality, Virtual Desktop)
==========================================================================================

What you need
  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  - A graphics card with OpenGL 4.3 (GeForce GTX 900 / Radeon RX 400 or newer)
  - A PC VR headset with motion controllers: Valve Index, HTC Vive, Windows Mixed Reality, or a Meta Quest over
    Quest Link / Air Link / Virtual Desktop
  - An OpenXR runtime set as active: SteamVR (Settings > OpenXR > Set SteamVR as OpenXR runtime) or the Meta
    Quest Link app. Project Arena uses whichever is active.
  - Internet for online play; a clear play space of about 2 x 2 m

Install
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example Documents\ProjectArena). Keep the "assets" folder beside the exe.
  2. Start SteamVR (or Quest Link) and put the headset on.
  3. Double-click ProjectArena.exe. A window on the desktop shows what the headset sees.
  To update, unzip the new version over this one (your settings are kept in %APPDATA%\ProjectArena).

Play together
  - Online (the default): MENU > MULTIPLAYER shows the rooms on the community server; Quest and PC players meet
    there. HOST A ROOM to open one, or JOIN someone's; in the waiting area press JOIN QUEUE (the red button) to play.
  - Same home network instead: MULTIPLAYER > ONLINE: OFF (LAN), then host and join the same way.
  - You start in the lobby over the practice court: point at a poster or the court and pull the trigger.
  - Two players start face to face in the centre: hold your trigger and bump fists (or wait 10 seconds).
  - Hold the left grip to talk. MENU > REPORT BUG sends us a note and the game's recent log; MENU > REPORT PLAYER
    reports or mutes someone in your room.

Controls
  Trigger        hold near your ball to grab it; swing and let go to throw
  Charged ball   holding one raises your shield; your fists punch balls away
  Menu           Index: left B.  Vive / WMR: left menu button.  Quest (Link): left menu button.
  Re-centre      Index: right B.  Vive / WMR: right menu button.  Quest: B.  Or click a thumbstick.
  Left grip      push to talk

If the game does not start, open %APPDATA%\ProjectArena\projectarena_log.txt (it says what went wrong).

Quest players can play standalone too: see README-Quest.txt on the release page.
