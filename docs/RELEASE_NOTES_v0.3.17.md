# Project Arena 0.3.17

## New
- **CREDITS** on the HOW TO PLAY page: the people who made Project Arena, and the community members who helped
  shape it.
- **BALL MARKS**: where a ball bounces off the floor, a wall or the glass, it leaves a faint mark (a small hot scorch
  with a pale glow) that fades over a few seconds, so you can see where a bounce landed and aim for it again. Everyone
  in the room sees them; HIT EFFECTS SOFT and LOW tone them down. A community idea.
- **The Crowd in the four-player arenas**: in FOUR CORNERS and FIREBALL BLITZ, the fans' cheers now pour hearts, stars
  and flags out of the stands, and their flags wave there, as on the classic court.

## Fixed
- **Country flags on the jersey** are printed on the cloth itself, on the back and the chest, and bend and turn with
  it. As flat cards they stuck out from the back like paper when you leaned or threw.
- **Glows and flashes after a restart**: after QUIT GAME and starting the game again, the fireballs' flames, their
  light on the floor and the hit flashes could show a country flag (or another picture) instead of a soft glow.
- **Quest 2** runs at 72 Hz, for steady frames in the busiest arenas. The other headsets stay at 90 Hz.
- **BALL LOOK-AHEAD** says what it covers: the button now reads BALL LOOK-AHEAD (1-V-1 ONLINE). It smooths the ball
  when you're the guest in a two-player room; four-player rooms and FIREBALL BLITZ don't use it yet.
- The unfinished cross-play test (EXPERIMENTAL) is no longer on the title page.

## For the store version
- The Meta Horizon Store version checks at start that your account owns the game, as every store game must. Your
  headset only asks Meta yes or no; nothing about it reaches our server. The GitHub, SideQuest and PC versions don't
  check.

0.3.14, 0.3.15, 0.3.16 and 0.3.17 play together online.
