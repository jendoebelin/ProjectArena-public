# Project Arena 0.3.7 (ALPHA test build)

Store build: versionCode 11, versionName 0.3.7. Everything in 0.3.6, plus:

## New
- **The Crowd:** fans can follow online matches live on their phones at **crowd.aftersparc.com** (Android and
  iPhone, no app store or sign-in needed; it can be added to the home screen). They see the score, the clock or round,
  both players' flags, this match's statistics (throws, accuracy, hits, strikes, blocks, catches, top speed) and their
  careers, and they cheer for an end, wave its flag and call who scores next.
- **The arena hears its fans:** a crowd sound that swells as they cheer, a roar when an end's hype runs high, that
  end's lights flaring with each burst of cheers, and the screen over the court showing how many fans are watching
  and each end's hype. Everyone in the room and the spectators hear and see it.
- **Flags in the stands:** each fan waving a flag on their phone raises one flag on a pole in that end's stands (the
  country's flag, or the end's colour), flying in the wind; the side stands fill first, then behind the end zone.
- **Cheer bursts:** rapid cheering pours hearts, stars and little flags up out of that end's stands.
- **SETTINGS > CROWD:** SOUND AND FLAGS (the default), FLAGS ONLY (no crowd sound), or OFF (no crowd sound, flags,
  bursts or flaring lights during matches).
- The screen over the court shows where to follow live (CROWD.AFTERSPARC.COM) when nobody is watching yet.
- Strikes are now counted for each player.

## Spectating on the PC
- VIEW LAYERS > KEY is always transparent now (for OBS Game Capture with Allow Transparency); the green, magenta and
  blue keys and the K key are gone (`--keycol` in old shortcuts is ignored).
- Game controller: LB / RB switch cameras too (as well as the D-pad); a camera picked during the fist bump or the
  winner close-up shows at once.

## Your data
When you host an online arena, your match (the players' names, countries, score and statistics) is shown live on
The Crowd's public web page while the arena is open; nothing about it is stored for the page. Fans are anonymous: no
sign-in, no name, and the server keeps nothing about them. Details: the privacy policy,
https://github.com/jendoebelin/ProjectArena-public/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md
