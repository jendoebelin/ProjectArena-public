# Project Arena 0.3.27

## New
- **THE GUTTER**: a curved cove where the side walls meet the floor, so the room's long edges are rounded instead of
  a sharp corner, and a ball comes off them as off a wall. **12 CM** is now the standard in SQUARE,
  DIAMOND and FOUR CORNERS; a host can choose OFF, 12, 25 or 40 CM. Everyone in the match needs 0.3.27 (with a
  0.3.26 player in it, OFF, and the host is told).
- **STRIKE BY TOUCH**: any part of the ball crossing inside a strike zone is a strike, with 10 cm to spare (from the
  far end, a ball just outside the edge looks as if it touched). With a 0.3.26 player in the match, as before: the
  ball's middle.
- **The strike zones, frosted** in their player's colour, exactly as big as they score. The back walls carry the end
  zone's hex pattern, faint, in their team's colour, so they read as walls, and the only rings left are the strike
  zones'.
- **Faces, builds and facial hair**: **FACE 5 and 6** on both bodies (four new faces, with their own tops and
  shorts, and four more hairstyles); **BUILD STRONG, LEAN and SOFT**, bodies of their own under any outfit;
  **FACIAL HAIR**: MUSTACHE, GOATEE, SHORT BEARD and LONG BEARD, in your hair colour, swaying as you move.
- **Two new outfits**: **DRAGON SILK QIPAO** (crimson silk, a gold dragon) and **CHERRY POODLE SKIRT** (a felt circle
  skirt that swishes and twirls as you move). With a country and MY OUTFIT, both take its colours. Players on older
  versions see you in GRID RUNNER, without facial hair (and, with facial hair, a SMOKE face shield tint).
- **SIDE COLOR**: your end of the arena and your ball in a colour of your own, without choosing a country (the
  builder, at the top of COLORS AND PATTERN). A country still colours your end with its own; change a colour the
  country decides, or SIDE COLOR, and you leave the country for a custom look of your own, as it was, without its
  flags. When both ends' colours are too alike, the home end (the host's) keeps its own and the away end takes its
  country's alternative colour, or the natural opposite. Players on older versions see the usual blue and orange.
- **Bots that move like players**: they turn into the balls they block, sink into low ones and lean toward them,
  and twist away from a ball they dodge. CHAMPION and UNBEATABLE aim their strikes nearer the zone's edge. A new bot
  comes at the start of every match, and **SIMPLE SETUP** has NEW BOT: EVERY MATCH / ON PUSH and NEW BOT NOW. The
  court's bots shift their weight at the hips, and the feminine tops follow through a little as you move (both
  community contributions).
- **THE ROPE**: a new victory for the bots. A hologram jump rope, turned in big circles from the shoulders (recorded
  from real players jumping rope), a jump each turn; the rope slaps down on the floor, bends along it and kicks up a
  few sparks. Players on older versions see the arms and the jumps without the rope.
- **New host options, all off unless chosen** (the standard game is unchanged): **FIRM** throws and **MAX EFFORT**
  punches, FULL EFFORT's power tuned apart for throws and for knuckle returns; **WALLS: CLASSIC** (bounces of another
  feel); **CURVE ASSIST**; and **AIM OFF WALLS**, an AIM ASSIST that counts bounces. Everyone in the match needs
  0.3.27 (with an older player in it, off, and the host is told).
- **HOVER TEXT**: point at an option on the SETTINGS pages or a room's options, and it says what it does.
- **GRAPHICS** you can see: FLOOR REFLECTIONS show the players and bots too, fading with depth so the floor reads as
  polished, where the legs end in a portal's glow; BLOOM is stronger; each effect is ON or OFF.
- **Hand tracking**: catching and the grab off the spawner reach a little further, your own throw can't be caught
  straight back, and throws are read only from moments your hand was seen. Controllers are unchanged.

## Better
- **A grown ball** stops at 2.75 times its size, and its glow stays close to it, so long matches keep their frame rate.
- **The frame-rate guard** steps down gently in a match: bloom first, then smoothing 4X to 2X, then off. It leaves
  menus and loading alone.
- **The avatar builder** draws its panels much faster.
- **Your own reflection** is left out of the floor (it cost the most and you can't see it).
- **Arms that swing**: an elbow no longer snaps from one side of the arm to the other, tucks in behind your back, turns
  inside out, or lifts up to your shoulder like a wing when your hand swings low behind your hip; it swings round
  as a real one does. Arms hanging by your sides sit closer to the body.

## Fixed

- **HANDS APART** was off after QUIT GAME and starting the game again.
- **NEW BOT: EVERY MATCH** gave the same bots in the same order after every launch on Quest.
- **The BANDANA** under SHADES and GOGGLES: its top went behind the nose, and its point was drawn up to twice its size.
- **HIGH PIGTAILS** crumpled under HEADPHONES, GOGGLES, HEADSET and HALO; they hang free now. Under those face shields'
  straps every hairstyle showed a band of bare scalp at the back.
- **The lobby's rail sign** stood behind you in the avatar builder's mirror: it steps aside while a menu is open, and
  the rail while the builder is.
