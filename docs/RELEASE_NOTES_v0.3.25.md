# Project Arena 0.3.25

## New
- **THROW POWER and KNUCKLE RETURNS** (a community request): two new options for the host, on the room page's
  **BOUNCES & POWER** (in FOUR CORNERS: **POWER**).
  - **THROW POWER: NATURAL**: the ball's speed follows your effort more like a real throw. A light toss goes slow,
    and a full throw still reaches top speed.
  - **KNUCKLE RETURNS: PUNCH**: your punch sets the return speed, not how fast the ball came in. A still fist drops
    the ball back softly, and a real punch sends it hard.
  - **REGULATION**, the default for both, plays as before.
  - In the SQUARE, DIAMOND and FOUR CORNERS, and in TRAINING's **MY OPTIONS**. They play when everyone in the match
    has 0.3.25 (with an older player in it, regulation, and the host is told). The bots throw as they always have. A
    match with either counts as custom in the stats.

0.3.14 to 0.3.25 play together online. From December 2, versions before 0.3.20 no longer play online: update to keep
playing.
