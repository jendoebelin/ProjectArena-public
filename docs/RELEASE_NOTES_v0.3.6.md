# Project Arena 0.3.6 (ALPHA test build)

Store build: versionCode 10, versionName 0.3.6. Everything in 0.3.5, plus:

## New
- **Play for your country:** CUSTOMIZE > PRESET > **COUNTRY FLAG**, then pick one of 202 countries (and, for fun,
  Earth and the Galaxy) in the COUNTRY row under it. You wear a team jersey in the flag's colours with the flag on
  your back and a small flag emblem on your chest, gauntlets to match, and your end of the court, your strike zone
  and your balls take the country's colour. Flags hang on the walls around the court, and the scoreboards show them.
- **Career statistics:** online matches against another player now build your career: wins, throw accuracy,
  blocks, top throw speed, hits and catches. A match counts when both players' games agree on the result.
- **MENU > MY STATS:** your career as a dashboard, and **DELETE MY STATS** to erase it from the server.
- **The screen over the court** shows both players' names, flags or team colours, the score and their careers.
- **VOTE TO REMOVE** (MENU > REPORT PLAYER): when more than half of the other people in an arena room vote, the player
  is removed from that arena for the rest of its session. The host can't be removed from their own arena.

## Spectating on the PC (no headset)
- **VS screen (M):** at the fist bump and the countdown, the two players' portraits face each other with VS between
  them and their statistics underneath. M shows it any time (and again turns it off).
- Country flags in the top banner, a "VS" card at the fist bump, and the winner's flag on the win screens.
- **Streaming kit** (`streaming/` in the PC zip and on GitHub): moving OBS backgrounds, camera guides for making your
  own, and a step-by-step OBS guide.

## Fixed
- The ARENA JERSEY's collar was ragged and high up the neck (it looked like a bow tie): now a crew neck with a smooth
  ribbed collar. The TRAINING TEE gets the same neckline, and the TANK TOP's and TEE's edges no longer splay.

## Your data
Statistics are new information the game keeps about you on our server: they are public in the game, kept under a
scrambled form of a random key your game makes (no account, no email), and you can delete them in MY STATS. The game
tells you this once, the first time you enter an online arena room. Details:
the privacy policy, https://github.com/jendoebelin/ProjectArena-public/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md
