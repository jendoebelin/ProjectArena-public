## Short description (one line)

A free, open-source VR ball-combat sport: throw, curve and bank your ball past your rival's shield.

## Description

Project Arena is a one-on-one VR sport. You stand in your end of a neon court, grab your ball and throw it at your
opponent. Bank it off the walls, curve it with a flick of the wrist, and block their shots with your shield.
Every strike makes your ball bigger; first to win the rounds takes the match.

- **Real throwing physics**: your hand speed, spin and aim decide the shot.
- **Play friends anywhere**: host or join on your home network, or meet online through a community server.
- **Arena rooms**: up to four more people wait outside the tank, queue for the next match and talk with the players.
  When a match ends, the queue steps in.
- **Voice chat built for the room**: push-to-talk by default, open mic if you prefer, and separate mutes for
  spectators, the other player or everyone.
- **Spectate and stream**: fly around any live match, place cameras, and let the auto director cut between them
  with smooth cross-fades. Share camera setups with your team.
- **Make it yours**: build an avatar from suits, patterns, colours, hair, visors, masks and headgear, Tron-style looks included.
- **Practice bot**: warm up any time, or let the bot fill in while you wait for an opponent.
- **Free and open source** (MIT licence): no ads, no accounts, no tracking.
- **[verify]** Cross-play with PC VR (SteamVR / Index / Link) using the free PC build.

Inspired by the classic VR arena sports of the early Quest and Rift days. Project Arena is an independent project and is
not affiliated with or endorsed by any other game or studio.
