# Project Arena 0.3.14

## Changed
- **The shot clock is on show.** A thin ring round each player's floor launchpad builds while their ball sits in their
  end, and turns red once it has run out: from then on every throw the other player gets past their foul line is an
  automatic strike, until the ball is thrown again (the original's rule, which this game always had but never showed:
  strikes seemed to come from nowhere). Nothing is shown while a clock is full. Both players see both rings.
- **READY UP in rooms for four** (FOUR CORNERS and FIREBALL BLITZ online): a match starts only when every person in
  the tank has pressed **A**. You see PRESS A WHEN READY in front of you, then who the match is waiting for, and READY
  over the others who are. Bots are always ready. (Before, the bots being ready started the match on its own, around
  people still finding their place.)
- **After every match in a room for four, everyone goes back to the waiting area** and joins the queue again to play
  (the next match used to start straight away with the same people).

## Fixed
- **Square fireballs** (and a blocky ball): after a while in the game, the ball's and fireballs' plasma broke into big
  square blocks on the headset. Their scrolling textures now keep their precision however long you play (so do the
  court's pulse, the crowd lights and the suits' pulsing lines).
- A player moved into a room for four's tank as a match started on its own stood at the arena's edge, unable to play,
  while it went on. Ready-up means nothing starts until they are in place.
