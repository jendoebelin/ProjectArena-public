# Project Arena 0.3.10 (ALPHA test build)

Store build: versionCode 14, versionName 0.3.10. Everything in 0.3.9, plus:

## New
- **A new practice bot.** It moves the way a person can: it steps, leans and ducks out of the way, reaches only as
  far as an arm does (no more stretched arms), and grabs, throws, blocks, punches and catches with whichever hand is
  nearer. It reads curves and bank shots, catches its own ball coming back, and stands its own way: one of six
  stances each match (KITTEN, RELAXED, BOXER, KARATE, MANTIS, MONKEY).
- **Choose how good it is** (PRACTICE > MATCH OPTIONS): ROOKIE, AMATEUR, PRO, ALL-STAR, CHAMPION or UNBEATABLE, on
  a slider. Every level keeps to human limits (reaction time, hand and body speed, reach); the higher ones read the
  ball better, react sooner and throw harder. UNBEATABLE reads every ball. Can you score one?
- **MATCH MY LEVEL**: the bot follows you, getting better when you score and easier when it does, for close
  matches.
- **FIST BUMP FIRST**: meet the bot in the centre for a fist bump before the match (the KARATE bot bows).
- **The last 10 seconds**: a soft tick each second at the end of a timed match or round.
- **RECORD MY MATCHES** (MENU > MY STATS, off unless you turn it on): your matches saved on your headset, to help
  tune the bot from real play if you choose to send one.

## Changed
- **Avatar shoulders**: they rise with your arms when you raise them overhead, instead of folding in; elbows turn
  out to the side.
- **A blocked ball** can't count against the player who blocked it for 0.35 s, the same grace as after a throw (a
  ball could bounce straight off a shield into its blocker).
- MY STATS: the note under the dashboard no longer runs under the DELETE MY STATS button.
