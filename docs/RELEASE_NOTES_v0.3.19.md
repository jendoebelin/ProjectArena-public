# Project Arena 0.3.19

## New
- **STRIKE SPEED** (the host's MATCH OPTIONS, beside SHIELDS): REGULATION (the original's, and the default), FAST or
  FASTER: how much quicker the ball gets with each strike. Matches on FAST or FASTER don't count as standard in the
  stats. Four-player rooms stay at regulation.
- **PLAYERS' NAMES**: click the left stick to show everyone's name over their head, the queue's too; click again to
  hide them. Recentering stays on B and the right stick.
- **DEFAULTS on every settings page**: press it twice to put that page back as it came. People you muted by name
  stay muted.
- **FIREBALL BLITZ: fireball echoes**: smaller, fainter copies follow each fireball, so you can see where it's been.
- **New sounds**: an arena crowd cheers when you win, and groans when you lose; a new shield break, made by a member
  of the community; a new wall bounce. The Crowd's fans now set off a short cheer each time they cheer, in place of
  the crowd that ran under the whole match, and the arena's background sound is quieter.
- **SOUND STUDIO**: new sounds to try and vote on, among them five glass shield breaks.

## Better
- **Catches when you're not hosting**: the host now judges your catch from where the ball was when you saw it, so
  returns land in your hand far more often in someone else's room.
- **Your own ball is always yours to catch**, whoever touched it last: catch it as it floats back after a block.
- **The rising ball can be grabbed** as it comes up from the spawner.
- **Voice chat**: the crowd and the win roar turn down while someone talks.
- **BALL LOOK-AHEAD** (off by default) glides more smoothly, near and far.

## Fixed
- **The view keeps its centre** when you take the headset off and on, or recenter with the Meta button.
- **Ball trails** were drawn as arrows while you waited in line, watched, or played in someone else's room.
- **TRAINING and the SOUND STUDIO** could change your MATCH OPTIONS (FIST BUMP FIRST, the knuckle guards). If
  yours changed, set them once more; they stay now.
- **The SOUND STUDIO** opens its page in front of you.
- **BALL MARKS on LOW** leave a mark on every other bounce (they stopped while one showed).
- **PC spectator**: the side-by-side screens always show both players.
- **The store button** in the game opens the store page.

0.3.14 to 0.3.19 play together online. The better catches need a 0.3.19 host.
