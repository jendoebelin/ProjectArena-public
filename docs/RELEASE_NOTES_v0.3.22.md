# Project Arena 0.3.22

## New
- **Graphics settings** (SETTINGS > GRAPHICS AND VIEW), each for your own headset only: FOVEATION (full detail in the
  middle of your view and less at its edges, where the lenses blur anyway; HIGH from the start, so the headset has more
  room), SMOOTH EDGES (no more shimmer on the court's lines, for headsets with room to spare), CONTACT SHADOWS under
  players and balls, COLOR GRADING, BLOOM and FLOOR REFLECTIONS. The last four can be on only while you spectate. If
  frames start dropping in a match, bloom and smooth edges switch themselves off until it ends. Nothing is sent: no one
  else's game or connection changes.
- **The PC spectator's graphics**: a GRAPHICS tab in Spectator Controls with the same effects plus AMBIENT OCCLUSION
  and a VIGNETTE, all on, for a richer stream at no cost to the players.
- **Two new PC spectator cameras** (ideas from a member of the community): **8 CORNER**, high over a corner of the
  room looking down its length, and **9 COURTSIDE**, low at the side looking straight across. Both are in AUTO (a list
  you've made yourself gets them at its end, unticked).
- **BOUNCES**, a room option in the SQUARE and DIAMOND (ideas from a member of the community): LIVELY walls, WALL GRIP
  (spin that grips the walls, floor and ceiling, and the knuckle guards) and BANK ASSIST for a throw off the wall.
  Standard by default; it plays when everyone in the match has 0.3.22.
- **Custom training**: TRAINING's MODE: MY OPTIONS plays the lessons with your own room options, to train for the
  rules you compete in. Those bests stay on your headset; the boards keep the standard lessons.
- **New outfits from the community**: the RONIN WRAP and RONIN KABUTO; the PELURU TUXEDO and LAYERED DRESS with the
  sombrero and hoop earrings; HIGH PIGTAILS and DREADLOCKS that swing; the GRAPPLER, ARC CLAW and REGALIA gauntlets,
  whose fingers open on the trigger; the bandana's tails now swing too.
- **Presets for the full sets**: RONIN, PELURU TUXEDO, PELURU DRESS, VECTOR and NIGHTFORGE.
- **Country kit**: with a country picked, MY OUTFIT wears your own suit in your country's colours, with its flags.
- **SOUND STUDIO**: the hard punch has its own slot, S34 HARD PUNCH.
- **The asset viewer** (jendoebelin.github.io/ProjectArena-public): every outfit, part, sound and texture in your
  browser, each with its download, and how to make your own.

## Better
- **Smoother in busy matches**: players across the room are drawn with lighter models (you can't tell at that
  distance), the floor's mirror image is left out while you play, and foveation is on: on a Quest 3 the graphics chip
  has about a fifth less to do in a four-player FIREBALL BLITZ.
- **Solid floors to the stands** in every room, with a light bar round the players' area as the SQUARE has: no more
  gaps behind the players, and FIREBALL BLITZ's waiting row stands on floor. DIAMOND still sits sunk in it.
- **The bots throw like people**: the hand turns with the arm through a throw (the forearm twisted round).
- **Customize avatar** follows the body: headgear at the top down to the lower body on one side, with each colour
  beside its part; presets, country, body type and build on the other.
- **Lessons** always play the standard rules, so every time on a board is comparable.
- **Clean shots from the PC spectator's fixed cameras**: the walls and the roof between the camera and the court are
  left out, so their lines no longer cross the picture (the floor's edge stays). BLUE END and ORANGE END now look down
  the court from inside the glass, above and behind the player.

## Fixed
- **The court's energy pulses** follow a moved FOUL LINE (they ran through the stands beside the court).
- **A ball in your left hand** sits in your palm as it does in the right (it sat off the back of the hand), and your
  throw starts from there. This applies when everyone in the match has 0.3.22.

0.3.14 to 0.3.22 play together online. BOUNCES needs everyone in the match on 0.3.22; players on older versions see
the new outfits as the standard ones until they update. As announced with 0.3.21, from December 2 versions before
0.3.20 no longer play online: update to keep playing.
