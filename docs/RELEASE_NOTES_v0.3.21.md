# Project Arena 0.3.21

## New
- **Rooms and their options**: HOST A ROOM and PRACTICE start with CHOOSE A ROOM, each room a picture: the SQUARE,
  DIAMOND, FOUR CORNERS and FIREBALL BLITZ. A room's page holds everything the host can set for it: a TIMED MATCH or
  ROUNDS (BEST OF 1 to 9: - and +, or type it), SHIELDS (ON, OFF for HOTDOG, or ALWAYS: MAIDEN, a shield that never
  breaks), SHIELD SIZE (REGULATION up to DOUBLE), KNUCKLES, AIM ASSIST (OFF, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH), STRIKE ZONE (SMALL,
  REGULATION, LARGE), STRIKE SPEED, BALL GROWTH, the time, and in the SQUARE the FOUL LINE (two steps shorter or
  longer). Lists and boards name the room and its match: SQUARE - BEST OF 3.
- **A new standard ball**: its glow as big as what hits, it turns with its spin as it flies, and it's heavier in your
  hand (it trails your hand a little, and the controller hums as you swing it). The host can change each of these, or
  give it a solid core, in BALL FEEL on the room's page.
- **Fair matches**: a rule plays only when everyone in the match has it. With a player on an older version, the room's
  new options play as regulation, and the host's countdown says so.
- **The bot's style**: BRAWLER or DUELLIST beside its level, in any match. It now catches its own ball when you send it
  back, and slaps it straight back at you.
- **Buttons**: SETTINGS > CONTROLLERS > BUTTONS gives each button its job, with a LEFT-HANDED preset; a grip can talk,
  peek, show the names, or do nothing. Push to talk can move to any button.
- **Controller angle** (SETTINGS > CONTROLLERS): set how your hands sit on the controllers (tilt, twist, turn).
- **A queue screen** beside your waiting spot: the players' names, the score and the strikes. Click the left stick to
  show or hide it.
- **New bodies and clothes**, made with a member of the community: new bodies and heads (FACE 1 to 4), LONG SHORTS,
  a TENNIS SKIRT and a KILT that swing as you move, and refitted helmets.
- **Arms that move like yours**: your shoulder follows a long reach, the chest turns and leans toward it, the arms
  size themselves to your reach (straight at full reach), and the forearm turns with your hand.
- **PC spectator**: AUTO ROTATION picked by the streamer, ARENA GLASS, and BLUE END and ORANGE END cameras from above.

## Better
- **BALL LOOK-AHEAD is on for everyone**: in a room someone else hosts, a throw leaves your hand at once, grabs and the
  shield are instant, and the ball moves smoothly. The host still decides every hit.
- **Blocks online**: a shield or knuckle guard block in someone else's room counts as you saw it.
- **Grabbing and punching**: take the ball from anywhere round your fist, with a cue when it's in reach; the knuckle
  guards sit on the fist; a punch lands with a moment of impact.
- **Round and match announcements** are bigger, on a backing plate.
- **The microphone** is asked for only the first time you go to talk, with a heads-up first, not as you enter a room.
- **Your reflection in the avatar builder** follows you, is placed in front of you again when you recenter, and shows
  the skirts swing.
- **CHOOSE A ROOM's pictures** are raised buttons.

## Fixed
- **Recentering with the Meta button** always puts the court in front of you.
- **The strike ring** is drawn exactly where a strike counts (it was drawn a little bigger).
- **A player back from sleep** no longer takes the room over.
- **The gauntlets**: the fist sits in line with the cuff, held together the two no longer cross into each other, and
  the elbows stay out of the body.
- **VANGUARD's plates** fit the new bodies (one smooth chest plate), and the jersey's hem and the skirts' waistband no
  longer flicker.
- **FIREBALL BLITZ**: moving between rooms goes through dark, not a cross-fade.
- **PC spectator**: DIAMOND after FIREBALL BLITZ no longer draws the players off their platforms.

0.3.14 to 0.3.21 play together online; the new room options and BALL FEEL need a 0.3.21 host. Two weeks after this
update, versions before 0.3.20 will no longer play online: update to keep playing.
