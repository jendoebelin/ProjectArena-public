# Project Arena 0.3.0 (test release)

A free 1-v-1 VR court sport for Meta Quest and PC VR: throw, curve and punch energy balls at your opponent in a neon
glass arena. Inspired by CCP Games' Sparc; an independent game with its own code, art and sound.

## New in 0.3.0
- **Online play** through the community server, on by default: Quest and PC players meet in the same rooms.
- **Rooms**: host a room, everyone starts in its waiting area and joins the queue to play; forfeit to step out;
  spectate any live match; voice chat (push to talk on the left grip) with automatic microphone levels.
- **Avatars** rebuilt from MPFB2 human bodies: two body types, outfits that drape like fabric, hair, headgear, face
  shields, masks, and medieval-style plate gauntlets (Striker, Titan, Phantom) or the classic controller glove.
- **Main menu** with your avatar on a pedestal; **REPORT BUG** in every menu.
- Ball rules closer to Sparc: swing through the ball on its spawner to grab it; short throws float back home.
- Recentring with the Meta button; menus that fit their buttons.
- PC VR version (SteamVR / Quest Link).
- Experimental: a spectate-only cross-play test with Sparc community servers (off unless you choose it).

## Coming later
- **Hand tracking**: an optional mode to play without controllers (menus, the avatar builder and casual practice
  first; controllers stay best for fast throws and punches).

## Known limits
- Test release: expect rough edges, and please report them (MENU → REPORT BUG).
- Quest 3 is the tested headset; Quest 2, 3S and Pro should work.
- The avatars end above the knee.
