Project Arena Asset Catalog

Every model, texture and sound made for Project Arena to replace CCP's library. All of it is generated by our own scripts; none of it comes from Sparc. Refer to anything by its ID, for example "P04 needs thinner fingers", "V14 hair too flat" or "S12 is too harsh", or send a replacement file for that ID.

Download the template pack Every card has a Download button for its model (.glb, opens in Blender) or texture. The template pack has the bodies, heads and hand frame to build new outfits, hair, masks and gauntlets on, plus how to submit them. Contributed art is published under CC BY 4.0.

Avatars V01 to V77 · models

Waist-up humans (no legs, like the original) built by tools/blender/make_avatar.py. In the game the arms follow your real hands, and a rim light in your team colour outlines every player. Open the builder from the menu: CUSTOMIZE AVATAR (practice only). Your look is saved in avatar.cfg and sent to the other player.
